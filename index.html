Normal map generator questions

What does a normal map generator actually do?

It analyzes the shading and detail in an image or mesh and encodes surface direction into an RGB texture. Game engines use that map to fake bumps, grooves and edges without adding polygons.

Is this normal map generator free?

You can generate models and preview textures on the free tier. 4K PBR textures with normal, roughness and metallic maps are part of the paid plans, which start at a few dollars per month.

Can I get a normal map from a single photo?

Yes. A flat photo of brick, wood, fabric or metal is enough to produce a usable normal map. For best results use even lighting and a straight-on angle.

How does the 3D text generator work?

You describe what you want, for example a chrome logo with rounded edges, and the model generates a textured mesh from that text. You can then generate a normal map for it or export it directly.

Does the Roblox mesh export work in Roblox Studio?

Yes. The export keeps the triangle count within Roblox limits and packs the textures so the mesh imports through the asset manager without manual cleanup.

Which formats can I download?

Textures export as PNG. Models export as GLB, FBX, OBJ, USDZ and STL, with the PBR maps included where the format supports them.
